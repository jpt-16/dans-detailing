Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

Dan's Detailing ("we," "us," or "our") provides mobile car detailing services in Framingham and the surrounding MetroWest, Massachusetts area. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information We Collect

When you book an appointment, we collect the information you provide directly to us through our booking calendar or by phone, text, or email — such as your name, phone number, email address, and service address. We do not collect payment card information through this website; payment is arranged directly with Dan (cash, Venmo, or card in person).

Booking Through Google Calendar

Appointments are booked through Google Calendar's appointment scheduling tool, embedded on this site. When you book a time, the information you enter is submitted directly to Google and shared with us to confirm and manage your appointment. Google's handling of that information is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to schedule and perform detailing services, communicate with you about appointments, and respond to questions. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Cookies & Analytics

This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Google Calendar and Google Fonts, which this site relies on, may set their own cookies or collect technical data (such as IP address) as part of their normal operation — see Google's privacy policy linked above for details.

Data Retention

We retain booking and contact information only as long as needed to provide our services and maintain basic business records.

Your Choices

You can ask us to review, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by contacting us using the details below.

Contact Us
